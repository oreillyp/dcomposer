Layout under the --root directory passed to scripts/evaluate.py:

inputs/<dataset>/audio/<id>.wav
inputs/<dataset>/transcript/<id>.mid|.txt|.json
inputs/synthetic/oneshot/<id>/*.wav
predictions/final_normm16_p095_fullamp_ae5/{transcripts,roundtrip,oneshots}/<dataset>/audio/...
predictions/<baseline>/transcription/preds/<dataset>/<id>.json
predictions/idm/roundtrip/<dataset>/<id>.wav
predictions/<baseline>/oneshot/synthetic/<class>/<id>.wav
codecs/inputs/00000.wav ... 01999.wav
codecs/reconstructions/{dac,codicodec,autoencoder,melodyflow}/00000.wav ... 01999.wav

Datasets: e_gmd, mdb_drums, synthetic, fsl10k (round-trip only).
Baselines: idm, dose, adtof.
Baseline hats use hihat. Transcripts are MIDI for E-GMD, text for MDB,
and JSON for synthetic. Full scoring expects 4,500
decomposition inputs and four sets of 2,000 codec reconstructions.
Both raw MSE and L1 spectral scores are written, alongside RMS-matched scores.
Scorers write summary JSON, per-recording CSVs, and Markdown/TeX tables without
requiring the paper source files.
